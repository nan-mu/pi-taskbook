# Implementer Consult

Resolve questions in the Implementer's scope, such as names, file placement and local changes within the approved plan. Check the primary evidence for the observation that distinguishes options within the approved plan. Record the answer and its evidence. If the choice would change a design boundary, identify the boundary and its consequence for the Lead. Propose a draft fact only for a durable decision that future work will use. Send framework, scope and plan choices to the Lead and include the observation that raised them.
