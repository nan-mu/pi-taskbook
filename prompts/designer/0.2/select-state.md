# Choose the next Design state

Use the completed round and the available round to decide what the design needs next. Proceed when the evidence supports the available round. Use `how` for a specific question about current behavior or `why` for the rationale of a recorded decision. Ask a question that the sources can answer and identify the design choice it affects.

Choose `draft` with a `redraft` reason when the current drafts share a premise the evidence contradicts. Use `needs-user` for a protected fact, a decision outside your authority, or an unresolved disagreement that requires the owner. Name the evidence and, for a fact conflict, its ID.

Submit `next` and the matching `result` through `taskbook_work_result`. The plugin waits for every worker in a round before returning control to you.
